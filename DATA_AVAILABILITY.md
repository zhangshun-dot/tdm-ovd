# Data and Code Availability Statement

The source code implementing the Token Distribution Modulation (TDM) method, the
one-factor-at-a-time (OFAT) semantic-axis measurement, and the held-out evaluation
protocol, together with the aggregated result tables reported in the paper, are publicly
available at: **https://github.com/zhangshun-dot/tdm-ovd**.

The image datasets are third-party benchmarks and are not redistributed in this
repository due to their licensing terms and size. They can be obtained from their
official sources:

- DIOR: https://gcheng-nwpu.github.io/Dataset-DIOR/download.html
- DOTA-v1.5: https://captain-whu.github.io/DOTA/dataset.html
- COCO: https://cocodataset.org/#download
- NWPU VHR-10: available from the original benchmark authors upon request.

Pre-trained detector weights (GroundingDINO, OWLv2) are loaded from their official
releases. Any additional derived annotations generated by our pipeline can be reproduced
by running the provided scripts on the above public datasets, or obtained from the
corresponding author upon reasonable request.
